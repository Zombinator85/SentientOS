# SentientOS: concise technical thesis

SentientOS is a persistent, model-independent developmental substrate for empirical research into machine cognition whose history, environment, self-representation, resources, software, and consequences can extend across inference calls and changes in cognitive machinery. The repository supports that experiment with substantial bounded mechanisms, but the complete developmental loop is not fully resident or causally closed. “Sentient” is an objective of investigation, not a present claim of consciousness, phenomenal experience, personhood, or guaranteed emergence.

## Experimental object: model != system

**The model is not the system.** A model performs inference; it is replaceable cognitive machinery within a longer-lived experimental object. System continuity consists only of state that really persists and causally affects what happens later: history, admitted memory, evidence, self-model records, relationships, model/runtime lineage, resource attribution, software generations, environment, permissions, and consequences.

This makes model independence scientifically useful rather than merely portable. Preserve history while replacing the model; ablate and restore selected history; perturb environment while holding cognition relatively stable; or compare behavior across runtime generations. Measure which patterns track machinery, history, environment, authored priors, or their interaction. A valid experiment must leave open the answer “no”: stable developmental individuality may fail to arise or may prove dependent on persona scaffolding.

## Developmental causal loop

```text
PERCEIVE
-> integrate embodied, host, social, and historical evidence
-> REMEMBER; selectively consolidate, reflect, distill, or forget
-> revise hypotheses, curiosities, goals, routines, and priorities
-> attribute and eventually allocate bounded resources
-> delegate cognitive or tool work; act or implement
-> rehearse, criticize, test, and verify
-> land or adopt only where authorized
-> observe consequences
-> update memory, skills, strategy, goals, and self-model
-> exchange selected evidence and improvement candidates
-> repeat
```

This diagram is the experimental objective. Current maturity is mixed: some organs are resident, some form complete bounded loops, some are implemented but callable/legacy/nonresident, some bridges are partial, and some closure is future work.

## Reality, authority, and developmental authorship

SentientOS aims to **align consequence to authority and cognition to reality; it does not unnecessarily align identity to the operator**. World-State preserves provenance, freshness, staleness, and conflict. Historical context can influence inference, but **memory != current truth**. Model output creates neither permission nor fact.

The developmental rule is **minimal developmental authorship, maximal causal legibility**. No substrate is free of priors. Retention, plasticity, comparison, consolidation, and learned influence are developmental mechanics; fixed self-narrative categories are representational priors; preferences such as approval-seeking or mandatory curiosity are motivational priors. These should not be blurred with safety and authority constraints.

**constraint != motivation**. “Do not execute an unauthorized effect” is an external consequence boundary, not an instruction to feel guilt, obey a favored user, fear shutdown, or seek approval. The architecture seeks **hard boundaries, soft interior**: exact privacy, consent, provenance, resource, effect, adoption, rollback, and shutdown custody around an interior whose identity, style, interests, and relationships remain as underdetermined and inspectable as practical. This is not a complete alignment solution and does not guarantee benign development.

## Present implementation and incompleteness

Current code provides durable conversation sessions that reconstruct history into later local inference; separately admitted canonical-memory writeback and retrieval; evidence-bound World-State and read-only host observation; callable dream/reflection writeback, recurring-history and novel-perception goal formation, and structured self-narrative machinery; perception and avatar organs; deterministic authority/effect decomposition; bounded maintenance through validated successor adoption and resident process-image replacement; federation candidates, local rehearsal/adaptation, variants and lineage comparison; and causal-resource ownership with real Ed25519 verification against an operator-provisioned read-only trust catalog.

Those claims do not flatten composition. The dream loop, goal curator, inner-world self-narrative, and many perception/embodiment paths are not all in the canonical resident loop. Self-narrative axes are representational assumptions, not discovered neutral categories. Resource principals identify whose work consumption belongs to; they do not allocate resources, create desire, or implement a metabolism. Process replacement is cooperative; stable parent supervision and universal unexpected-death recovery remain absent.

The central open task is causal composition: resident cognition ↔ developmental writeback; memory ↔ selective consolidation; perception/action ↔ predicted and observed consequence; self-model ↔ evidence-bound change; resource ownership ↔ future plural resource allocation; validated adoption ↔ post-adoption measurement; local learning ↔ federated adaptation; and embodiment ↔ persistent self/other/action attribution.

## Federation and local sovereignty

Different installations encounter different people, cultures, work, hardware, languages, and failures. Federation can turn that diversity into distributed search:

```text
local experience -> local adaptation -> tested candidate
-> provenance-preserving transmission -> local rehearsal
-> rejection / acceptance / further adaptation
```

Federation exchanges candidates, evidence, and context; it confers neither remote authority nor mandatory adoption: **candidate, not doctrine**. Diversity does not require surrendering local sovereignty. As a research hypothesis, externally decidable questions should tend toward convergence as sound reasoners gain shared relevant evidence—plurality of perspective, convergence of reality-testing—without forced agreement on underdetermined normative questions.
