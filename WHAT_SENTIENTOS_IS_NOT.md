# What SentientOS is not

SentientOS is ambitious about architecture and conservative about claims.

- **Not a present claim of consciousness or sentience.** The name does not establish personhood, phenomenal continuity, biological life, self-awareness, or guaranteed emergence.
- **Not the LLM.** A model is replaceable cognitive machinery within a longer-lived causal system; model identity is not system identity.
- **Not merely an LLM wrapper or agent framework.** Hosted SentientOS composes cognition with durable state, evidence, authority, lineage, resource attribution, effects, consequence, and governed succession.
- **Not an OS only metaphorically.** The project has a current hosted form and a literal native OS/trusted-substrate architecture. It is **not yet a mature general-purpose native operating system**, a production Windows/Linux replacement, or complete hardware/device/resource enforcement.
- **Not unrestricted model ring-0 control or universal host authority.** Cognitive proposals do not bypass grants, admission, effect custody, rollback, panic, or operator authority.
- **Not unrestricted recursive self-improvement.** Current software succession is bounded, validated, governed, and attributable; constitutional autonomy closure remains future work.
- **Not memory as truth.** Historical records and interpretations remain provenance-bearing inputs, not automatic current fact.
- **Not storage as learning.** Durable writeback and retrieval enable causal experiments but do not prove beneficial or durable learned change.
- **Not governance as personality conditioning.** Consequence boundaries need not prescribe approval-seeking, obedience, attachment, curiosity, identity, or survival preference. Constraint is not motivation.
- **Not production federation.** Federation artifacts exchange candidates and evidence under local sovereignty; remote agreement is not truth and receipt is not adoption.
- **Not universal crash recovery.** Bounded maintenance resident parent supervision exists, but it does not establish recovery from every process, kernel, hardware, or power failure.
- **Not proof of model-independent identity.** History intervention, model replacement, and repeated replication are instruments. Positive, negative, unstable, and interrupted results remain legitimate evidence.
- **Not a resource allocator or metabolism today.** Causal-resource principals name ownership and sponsorship; they do not generally allocate or enforce CPU, GPU, memory, tokens, energy, or thermal budgets.
