# Sequence 636 - present bounded peer Health claims to operators

Sequence 636 exposes the daemon's existing peer Health projection through RuntimeShell and the console dashboard. It labels the data explicitly as peer-reported claims, separate from local Health. The projection includes only a peer label, recovery outcome, allowed peer-reported status, positive sequence, freshness state, and bounded error code; endpoints, signing key IDs, and digests stay out of the operator view. The normalizer caps results at 64 entries and the console displays at most three. With no configured source, RuntimeShell returns an empty map. This adds no peer transport, production identity, trust provisioning, local Health publication, admission, planning, execution, or decision authority.
