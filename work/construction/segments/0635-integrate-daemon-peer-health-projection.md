# Sequence 635 - integrate daemon peer Health projections

Sequence 635 connects restart-safe authenticated peer Health recovery to the federation poller. A configured peer can receive a separate recovered projection from its caller-provided replay owner, pinned trust catalog, and signature verifier. The poller keeps the projection separate from local Health, federation peer reports, replay events, callbacks, admission, planning, execution, and decision authority. It reports `empty`, `omitted`, and bounded `error` outcomes distinctly; recovered observations must match the configured peer identity. Healthy and adverse (`unavailable`) outcomes are both preserved.

No peer transport, production identity, owner provisioning, or trust-catalog rotation policy is enabled by this increment. The runtime currently constructs the poller without peer Health sources, so configured peers remain explicitly omitted until an operator-provisioned source is safely connected. This prevents a test seam from becoming implicit production trust.
