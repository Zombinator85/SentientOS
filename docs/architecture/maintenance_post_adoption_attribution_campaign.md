# Repeated post-adoption attribution campaigns

`MaintenancePostAdoptionAttributionCampaignOwner` is a small composition layer above, not a replacement for, `MaintenancePostAdoptionEvaluationOwner`. It preregisters a content-addressed campaign of 2–32 ordered trials and binds the exact maintenance task/proposal/signal lineage, predecessor and successor generation/revision/tree, adoption identity, underlying one-shot evaluation protocol identities and digests, target observables and measurement laws, protected invariants, control definitions and matching laws, attribution rule, evidence class, trial inventory, and all-false authority.

Every trial is terminal exactly once. Interrupted, invalid, contradicted, null, mixed, confounded, measurement-failed, and insufficient-evidence trials cannot be retried, replaced, omitted, deleted, or filtered from finalization. Finalization requires the entire preregistered order. Immutable content-addressed protocol, control, trial, result, and signal records reconstruct with identical identities after restart and fail closed on changed laws, lineage, authority, or bytes.

## Independent controls and vocabulary

An admissible control is an independently collected World-State evidence record, host observation, external instrument record, operator-attested observation, independent workload replay, or contemporaneous reference. It retains source identity/digest/schema/class, observation time, collector, dependencies, matched-window identity, and available World-State/host bindings. Model narration, evaluator output, and the changed software's own environmental claim are not admissible source classes. A production campaign accepts only production-class controls; synthetic fixtures remain synthetic and set `production_ready=False`.

The categorical results are `repeated_association_under_matched_controls`, `repeated_target_contradiction_under_matched_controls`, `environmental_confound_detected`, `heterogeneous_repeated_outcome`, `no_detectable_target_change`, `insufficient_target_evidence`, `insufficient_control_evidence`, `measurement_failure`, `interrupted_or_invalid_trial`, `protected_regression_observed`, and `indeterminate`. Protected regression remains first-class. Mixed trials are never majority-voted. The strongest posture is always `repeated_controlled_association_not_experimental_causation`: observed matching can constrain a confound question, but it is not randomized causal identification.

The protocol names stronger future designs—independent workload replay, contemporaneous reference observation, and randomized intervention—but their names do not satisfy them. This implementation structurally closes the independently controlled repeated-production-observation bridge; no real production campaign is supplied by repository tests.

## Downstream boundaries

The epistemic adapter emits an ordinary proposition-specific source binding carrying the exact campaign/result/evaluation/control lineage. It never commits a proposition state; only `PersistentEpistemicStateOwner` may do that. The developmental adapter exposes immutable selectable evidence and performs no interpretation. Campaign-level repeated contradiction, regression, heterogeneity, confounding, or telemetry insufficiency can enter `governed_improvement_signal_plane` as a distinct proposal-only finding; adequately controlled satisfaction emits no recursion-for-recursion's-sake signal.

The owner has no cadence, ambient latest discovery, provider/network invocation, repository or Git mutation, adoption, process, scheduling, host-actuation, grant, admission, or rollback authority. It emits no rollback execution and cannot turn evidence into belief, truth, policy, or authority. Repeated association does not establish beneficial or monotonic recursive self-improvement, autonomous self-improvement, unrestricted RSI, consciousness, sentience, personhood, or phenomenal continuity. The next causal bridge is an operator-authorized real production campaign with independently collected controls; beyond that, a genuinely qualifying intervention/control design is required for experimental causal identification.
