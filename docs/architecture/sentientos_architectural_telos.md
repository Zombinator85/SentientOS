# SentientOS as a whole system

> **Posture:** evidence-grounded architectural synthesis, not authority. This
> document grants no capability and makes no claim of consciousness, sentience,
> autonomous intent, or completed recursive self-improvement. **ESTABLISHED**
> means demonstrated by present repository composition or bounded executable
> evidence; **IMPLIED** means a consequence of composing established mechanisms
> that has not itself been demonstrated; **HYPOTHESIS** means substantial
> implementation or empirical work remains.

This is the conceptual companion to the exhaustive
[Current Repository System Atlas](current_repository_system_atlas.md). The atlas
answers *what is in the tree and what is actually composed*. This document asks
a narrower but deeper question: **what kind of system do those mechanisms form
when their causal loops are connected and closed?**

## The answer in one sentence

**ESTABLISHED —** SentientOS is an operator-governed, persistent causal system
that keeps cognition, evidence, memory, authority, effects, software generation,
and model generation distinguishable so that some of them can change without
allowing any one of them to silently redefine the others.

**IMPLIED —** If its currently separate developmental, proof, maintenance,
model-transition, and consequence paths are closed, it becomes an experimentally
inspectable developmental runtime: one that can be changed partly through its own
cognition while preserving externally checkable continuity and attribution.

That is not a claim that the closure exists today. The current daemon does not
mint goals or intent, most consequential paths are opt-in, external model use is
unavailable by default, host observation is read-only by default, developmental
comparisons are bounded observations, and operator adoption remains decisive.

## How to read the claims

The three labels apply to every substantive projection below:

* **ESTABLISHED:** directly supported by current code, composition, tests, or
  durable evidence contracts. It may still require explicit configuration,
  authority, hardware, or operator action stated in the claim.
* **IMPLIED:** follows reasonably if named existing mechanisms are connected as
  their contracts anticipate. It is not a statement that the integration has run.
* **HYPOTHESIS:** a plausible qualitative property for which implementation or
  empirical evidence is too incomplete to infer closure.

“Implemented,” “composed,” “enabled,” “automatically exercised,” “authorized,”
and “effect occurred” remain separate predicates. A proof artifact proves only
what its producer and verifier bind. A model output is not authority. A receipt
is not necessarily effect proof. Remembered interpretation is not current truth.

## 1. The persistent object is causal organization, not a model

The architecture is easiest to understand by identifying what it refuses to
collapse. It maintains separate identities and custody for:

1. the system/runtime and its resident generation;
2. the repository and software generation;
3. the active cognitive model and model artifact;
4. reported model-development or training lineage;
5. developmental history and canonical user memory;
6. current environmental evidence and derived World-State;
7. operator authority, grants, admission, and adoption;
8. attempted effects, observed outcomes, and audit evidence; and
9. emerging causal-resource sponsorship.

**ESTABLISHED —** Model identity is explicitly not system, runtime, or
configuration identity. The local model path separates catalog, custody,
commissioning, activation, serving, and per-generation inference admission.
Developmental experiments bind model artifact/configuration identity separately
from current evidence, retained history, repository generation, and model-
development provenance. Maintenance succession separately binds Git state,
authority continuity, execution handoff, and final resident readiness.

**IMPLIED —** The identity-bearing object is therefore not a particular set of
weights. It is the continuing, attributable organization of state, authority,
history, software, cognition, and effects. A model can be replaced while that
organization persists; software can be succeeded while its causal lineage and
authority remain inspectable.

**HYPOTHESIS —** Whether any psychologically meaningful individuality persists
across replacements is an empirical question, not an architectural conclusion.
The repository supplies controls for asking bounded causal questions; it does
not establish selfhood or consciousness.

## 2. The major architectural families and what they become together

### 2.1 Current evidence, proof, and World-State: an incipient proprioceptive substrate

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** Tests, audits, immutable receipts, proof bundles,
host observations, and domain ledgers produce typed evidence. The World-State
Evidence Board builds a bounded deterministic read-only projection with source
provenance, freshness, lifecycle stages, conflicts, and deltas. The resident
daemon builds that board each maintenance tick. It is neither omniscient truth
nor an authority source.

**Why it exists.** Raw logs cannot support trustworthy self-observation if facts,
claims, stage, freshness, and conflict are indistinguishable. The board turns
heterogeneous evidence into a stable observation surface without letting the
observer grant itself permission or rewrite the observation by narration.

**Composition — ESTABLISHED, bounded.** Optional resident developmental cognition
receives the exact in-memory World-State snapshot for the tick, deterministically
selects allowed facts, preserves payload/provenance/conflicts, and records an
observation bound to the snapshot, model, request, receipt, and retrieved history.
Thus a real proof/evidence-to-cognition path exists, but only for configured,
bounded cognition and without action, policy, or self-model mutation.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** World-State plus durable proof plus cognitive
consumption is a machine-readable self-observation substrate: a form of bounded
proprioception about software, host, governance, and lifecycle condition. It is
“self” observation only where the evidence actually describes the running
system; it is never universal introspection.

**Missing bridge.** Architectural and operational: broaden authenticated source
coverage, normalize legacy provenance/freshness, and compose consumers that use
proof-derived observations in later diagnosis and proposal while retaining the
read-only boundary. Empirical: show that cognition responds to true controlled
changes rather than artifact wording or order.

**Closure evidence.** A preregistered run should induce a known system-state
change, produce independently verified evidence and a World-State delta, bind
the exact projection consumed by cognition, show the expected bounded change in
diagnosis, restore the prior condition, and demonstrate restoration stability.

### 2.2 Memory distillation and developmental history: continuity without treating memory as truth

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** Canonical conversation memory is a governed live
mutation path. Separately, selective memory distillation deterministically emits
retain, capsule, protect, defer, reject, and tomb-intent decisions but does not
write or delete memory. Receipt and tomb verification layers preserve the
difference between recommendation and mutation. Resident developmental writeback
can append immutable records under its own configured root; a later tick can
retrieve only bounded records from earlier completed ticks. Current World-State
remains separate from retained interpretation.

**Why it exists.** Persistence alone accumulates noise. Distillation expresses
what may remain salient; immutable developmental records preserve what influenced
later cognition; current evidence prevents remembered interpretation from
masquerading as present fact. Separate canonical-user and developmental roots
avoid silently widening retention semantics.

**Composition — ESTABLISHED, bounded.** The resident developmental path enforces
a temporal boundary: tick N cannot consume the record it creates on tick N. Its
optional intervention protocol compares history-present, history-withheld, and
history-restored cognition against the same frozen current evidence and endpoint.
It reports digest-level associations, not improvement or learning.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** If admitted distillation feeds this immutable
history and later cognition repeatedly consumes it, persistence becomes
longitudinal developmental continuity: later processing is causally conditioned
by attributable earlier experience while current truth remains externally
refreshable. The important property is not remembering more; it is knowing
which retained past participated in a later result.

**Missing bridge.** Architectural: an admitted writer from verified distillation
receipts into the developmental store and a governed relevance/retrieval policy
that is not merely fixed recent history. Operational and empirical: sustained
resident trials showing restart continuity and controlled history effects.
Constitutional: explicit retention, forgetting, privacy, and operator-consent
authority for any widened live use.

**Closure evidence.** Exact source record → distillation decision → admission →
write receipt → immutable history record → later retrieval → request/response
receipt chains, plus matched history-withheld/restored controls over many
predeclared ticks. This would establish causal use of attributable history, not
that the influence is beneficial or constitutes learning.

### 2.3 Improvement signals and software succession: governed recursive modification

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** The improvement signal plane turns test, coverage,
mypy, covenant, telemetry, capability-gap, and related evidence into deterministic
proposal-only signals. The maintenance chain can select a candidate; admit an
operator-derived lease; allow stochastic Codex/local-model machinery to author
inside an isolated worktree; independently reconstruct the diff; run fixed
validation; create a detached commit; locally absorb it by exact compare-and-swap;
derive same-or-narrower authority continuity; quiesce the predecessor; replace
the process image; and require post-exec successor readiness. These stages are
configuration- and authority-gated, POSIX-dependent for live replacement, and
not equivalent to a generally autonomous default loop.

**Why it exists.** A persistent system that can help author its successor needs
an adoption mechanism the authoring cognition does not control. Otherwise a
plausible patch, self-reported test result, or candidate process could declare
itself successful. The chain locates authoritative transitions in deterministic
controllers and operator-derived custody rather than model prose.

**Composition — ESTABLISHED for the traced bounded chain.** Stochastic proposal
and authorship are separated from deterministic measurement, repository adoption,
authority inheritance, process replacement, and successor readiness. Proof and
transition journals survive the cognition and process generation they describe.
Automatic rollback after absorption or replacement is not universal.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** Connected to proof-derived deficiency detection
and post-successor World-State, this is a recursive software-improvement loop:
observe deficiency → propose → author candidate → independently validate → adopt
software generation → become the successor → observe the resulting generation.
“Recursive” here describes causal topology, not autonomous goal generation,
unbounded iteration, guaranteed improvement, or self-authorization.

**Missing bridge.** Operational: one configured end-to-end resident campaign
binding the originating proof signal through successor postcondition and next
observation. Architectural: explicit evaluation of actual consequence relative
to the original deficiency, plus safe recovery/rollback for failed adopted
generations. Constitutional: durable operator policy governing repeated cycles.

**Closure evidence.** A single lineage must bind initial failing observation,
signal, proposal, lease, candidate diff, independent test outcomes, exact adopted
commit, continuity receipt, process transition, successor readiness, and a fresh
post-adoption measurement that resolves—or truthfully fails to resolve—the
original condition. Multiple preregistered repetitions are needed before claiming
reliable improvement.

### 2.4 Cognitive-model succession: replaceable cognition under persistent custody

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** Local cognitive artifacts have explicit provenance,
commissioning, activation, serving, and inference admission. The developmental
model-replacement instrument freezes current World-State, developmental history,
generation posture, budgets, and prompt construction while comparing two distinct
pre-governed endpoints under history-present and history-withheld conditions,
then restores model A. A campaign can repeat the same preregistered five-condition
experiment. Under explicit configuration, the resident path now also has hardened
serving, a stable slot and process-local quiescence gate, preregistered stages with
exact stage and activation approvals, canonical activation and serving owners,
transition-only rebinding, a durable installation-relative journal, explicit
operator ingress, and actual `sentientosd` composition. Synthetic live temporal
A→B→A evidence exercises the same slot, gate, control plane, and developmental
history identity. It is not production evidence from commissioned nonsynthetic
models, and autonomous model choice/acquisition remains unestablished.

**Why it exists.** If cognitive machinery is replaceable, model changes can be
treated as interventions rather than identity annihilation or silent upgrades.
Keeping history, current evidence, operational authority, software generation,
and reported training lineage independent creates controls that ordinary model
comparison lacks.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** With real resident transitions, the system could
experimentally distinguish effects associated with retained developmental history
from effects associated with the model artifact, software generation, current
environment, or their interaction. Persistent organization becomes an experimental
platform for cognitive succession rather than a wrapper around one permanent model.

**Missing bridge.** Operational and empirical: production temporal resident
transitions with commissioned nonsynthetic models, followed by replicated semantic
outcome measures rather than output-digest difference alone. Constitutional:
operator policy for selecting, commissioning, reverting, and retiring cognition.

**Closure evidence.** Predeclared factorial trials independently varying model,
history, software generation, and environment; exact endpoint and artifact custody;
semantic measures evaluated outside the compared model; stable restoration; and
longitudinal replication. Even then, persistence of “identity” would remain a
hypothesis unless operationally defined and independently measured.

### 2.5 Reflection and self-judgment: trustworthy only when grounded outside the narrator

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** Callable cognition includes reflection/narration,
simulation, and integration. A deterministic longitudinal self-model owner now
projects a narrow factual vocabulary from validated World-State into immutable,
digest-chained reconciliations with exact claim/source bindings, staleness,
contradiction, withdrawal, and supersession semantics. The older Glow mutable
self-state is compatibility scratch state, not this evidence plane. The new owner
is not yet a default resident writer or cognitive input. Developmental observations
explicitly perform no self-model mutation, goal creation, policy change, or action.

**Why it exists.** Free-form reflection is useful for interpretation and proposal,
but cannot be the sole measurement of a system participating in its own change.
The architecture therefore places proof, current evidence, admissions, and
consequences outside the narrator's authority.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** Reflection becomes trustworthy self-reflection
only when a statement about the system cites immutable observations, exposes
uncertainty/conflict, remains distinguishable from current truth, and can be
checked after both software and cognitive replacement. The trust belongs to the
chain of custody, not to introspective eloquence.

**Missing bridge.** Architectural/operational: explicitly configured runtime
composition after same-tick World-State, followed by bounded prior-tick cognitive
consumption that cannot recursively certify itself. Empirical: calibration tests
comparing self-assessment with externally measured condition and outcome.

**Closure evidence.** Controlled blind faults and recoveries, preregistered
expected observations, claim-level source bindings, measured false-positive and
false-negative rates, and continuity of interpretation across software/model
transitions. Success would establish calibrated bounded self-observation, not
privileged access to an infallible inner state.

### 2.6 Governance and effect custody: a hard shell around a developmental interior

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** Capability definition, grant, policy, feasibility,
admission, execution custody, attempt, effect proof, rollback, receipt, and
adoption are explicitly separated in bounded domains. The operator remains the
authority source; the daemon introduces no background intent. Real filesystem,
diagnostic, subprocess, process, model, and network mechanisms exist with
domain-specific gates, but most are not resident defaults. World-State display,
memory, model confidence, and proposal status confer no authority.

**Why it exists.** The cognitive interior is intentionally allowed to be
stochastic, revisable, and developmental. Safety therefore cannot depend on
making every thought deterministic. It depends on deterministic boundaries that
decide which proposals may cross into state mutation, physical effect, or
successor adoption and on evidence that records what actually crossed.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** This division permits interior cognition to
change—including replacement of the model or software that produced a proposal—
without automatically changing constitutional authority. It also permits a
failure to remain attributable: intention, authorization, attempted execution,
observed consequence, and adoption can disagree without being collapsed into a
single “success” bit.

**Missing bridge.** Coverage, not one universal gate: each new effect domain
needs exact authority, current feasibility, custody, rollback where possible,
consequence observation, and adoption semantics. Legacy paths need reconciliation.
Any widening of autonomous intent, live actuation, memory use, or provider egress
is a constitutional change, not integration cleanup.

**Closure evidence.** Adversarial tests and live bounded demonstrations showing
that proposals, stale grants, forged receipts, model claims, and partial attempts
cannot advance stages; exact authorized effects and rollback are observed; and
post-effect evidence returns through World-State without becoming self-authority.

### 2.7 Causal resource principals: from semantic sponsorship toward physical accountability

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** A thin causal resource principal identifies a
sponsored causal activity and has one bounded proof-budget producer/consumer
composition. It allocates nothing. Host resource telemetry observes CPU, memory,
disk, service, and thermal condition but is not causally metered per principal.
Effect admissions, component budgets, model limits, and resource-principal
identity remain separate.

**Why it exists.** Correlation IDs answer “which records look related”; effect
authority answers “may this action occur”; a causal resource principal answers
“on whose sponsored causal path is this work being done?” That semantic identity
is required before heterogeneous allocators can account for consumption without
turning resource availability into permission.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** If the same verified principal propagates through
planning, inference, execution, physical resource use, effect, consequence, and
later adaptation, the system can attribute cost and consequence to purpose rather
than merely to a process. It could then learn that an authorized strategy was
inefficient or harmful without confusing scarcity with prohibition.

**Missing bridge.** Architectural: child propagation, independent resource-class
allocators, pre-effect enforcement, consumption receipts, crash/retry reconciliation,
and cross-links to effect receipts. Physical: trustworthy meters for CPU/GPU,
energy, thermal, storage, or device use. Operational: resident propagation.
Empirical: calibrated attribution under shared work and batching.

**Closure evidence.** One principal-bound trace from sponsor evidence through
allocation and enforced execution to independently measured consumption, exact
effect receipt, observed consequence, and later cognitive use; conservation and
retry tests; and explicit uncertainty where physical attribution is approximate.

### 2.8 Federation and proof portability: continuity beyond one process is not yet one system

**What exists — ESTABLISHED.** Node identity, trust epochs, replay, federation
receipts, and laboratory WAN paths exist, while supported production WAN
synchronization is not established. Improvement candidates and lineage comparison
artifacts are proposals/evidence, not remote adoption or local authority.

**Why it exists.** Externally anchored proof and independently scoped local
authority permit evidence or candidate knowledge to travel without causing a
remote node to surrender adoption authority.

**Emergent property — IMPLIED.** If transport, verification, local review, and
local adoption close, improvements could be compared and disseminated while each
operator retains causal and constitutional custody. This is federated evidence
exchange, not a global autonomous mind.

**Missing bridge and evidence.** A supported authenticated transport deployment,
replay/currentness handling, receiving-node policy, and end-to-end demonstrations
from foreign evidence to explicitly local decision. Multi-operator consensus,
distributed identity, and production federation remain **HYPOTHESIS** unless
separately governed and observed.

## 3. Deep design invariants

The recurring invariants are not independent style preferences. They are the
conditions required for a changeable cognitive system to remain attributable.

| Invariant | Finding | Why the evidence supports it |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent causal organization rather than model identity | **IMPLIED**, strongly | Runtime, repository, model, history, authority, and provenance identities are independently bound; psychological identity is not established. |
| Replaceable cognition | **ESTABLISHED**, bounded | Model artifacts are commissioned/activated/served separately and controlled replacement experiments exist; real resident hot succession remains incomplete. |
| Externally anchored causal history | **ESTABLISHED**, domain-bounded | Immutable/hash-linked receipts, journals, Git lineage, admissions, and developmental records persist outside model output. Legacy coverage is not universal. |
| Proof survives the cognition it describes | **ESTABLISHED**, bounded | Proof and transition artifacts are durable inputs to deterministic verifiers and successor reconstruction. |
| Self-observation separated from self-judgment | **ESTABLISHED** as architecture; **IMPLIED** as whole-system property | World-State is deterministic/read-only while cognition interprets projections. A unified longitudinal self-model is not closed. |
| Stochastic proposal separated from deterministic measurement | **ESTABLISHED** on maintenance and governed effect paths | Models may author/propose; controllers reconstruct diffs, run tests, verify custody, and own adoption. |
| Current truth separated from remembered interpretation | **ESTABLISHED** | World-State projections and developmental/canonical memory have different roots, authority, freshness, and prompt roles. |
| Authority separated from belief | **ESTABLISHED** | Model confidence, memory, proposals, and display never substitute for grant/admission/operator authority. |
| Consequence separated from intention | **ESTABLISHED**, domain-bounded | Proposal, admission, attempt, completion, proof, receipt, rollback, and adoption are distinct states. End-to-end consequence feedback is incomplete. |
| Independently variable causal dimensions | **ESTABLISHED** in experiment contracts | Model, training provenance, history, current evidence, software generation, and environment are separately named; not all have been jointly manipulated. |
| Hard boundary around a soft developmental interior | **IMPLIED**, strongly | Deterministic custody encloses stochastic cognition and optional development. Boundary completeness across legacy paths is not universal. |
| Semantic meaning retained into execution | **ESTABLISHED** for exact admitted domains; **IMPLIED** for resources | Capability/effect identities survive to custody and receipts. Resource-purpose identity has not reached physical allocation. |
| Measurement not trivially rewritten by the measured proposal | **ESTABLISHED** on traced maintenance/proof paths | Independent test commands, immutable evidence, controller-owned state, and successor checks stand outside candidate prose; repository code can still change via later separately validated generations. |

### Why these invariants co-occur

The architecture permits three unusually powerful forms of change: remembered
experience may shape later cognition, cognitive machinery may be replaced, and
the resident software may adopt a successor partly authored by stochastic
cognition. Any one of these makes a single undifferentiated “agent state” unsafe
and scientifically opaque. If memory were truth, old interpretation could overrule
new observation. If belief were authority, a model could self-authorize. If a
candidate validated itself, recursion would erase its measuring stick. If model
identity were system identity, replacement would destroy the subject of comparison.

The separations therefore co-occur because they jointly preserve **causal
identifiability under change**. The architecture keeps the interior flexible by
making crossings—memory write, effect, model transition, repository adoption,
and resident succession—explicit, narrow, and evidenced. Governance is not an
adjacent safety feature; it is what makes developmental recursion inspectable
rather than merely self-referential.

## 4. Qualitative phase changes

These are transitions where a small number of bridges would change the system's
kind, not just add a feature.

### Phase change A — stored history → attributable developmental continuity

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** immutable developmental records,
   bounded later-tick retrieval, current-evidence separation, write receipts,
   history intervention, and selective-distillation decisions.
2. **Missing bridge:** admitted distillation-to-history write plus sustained
   resident retrieval and privacy/retention policy.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** later cognition is demonstrably conditioned by
   selected attributable history across restarts and model generations.
4. **Still unproven:** usefulness, semantic learning, stable values, identity,
   or beneficial development.
5. **Evidence:** end-to-end receipt chains and preregistered withheld/restored
   longitudinal controls with independent outcome measures.

### Phase change B — diagnostics → bounded machine self-observation

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** audits, host evidence, proof bundles,
   World-State conflicts/freshness/deltas, and a configured cognitive consumer.
2. **Missing bridge:** broader authenticated source coverage and calibrated
   claim-level reconciliation into a persistent self-model.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** the runtime can form evidence-cited, revisable
   representations of its own condition rather than merely display diagnostics.
4. **Still unproven:** completeness, privileged introspection, or accurate
   interpretation outside measured domains.
5. **Evidence:** injected-fault studies, source-to-claim lineage, calibration,
   stale/conflict behavior, and restoration tests.

### Phase change C — patch production → recursive resident improvement

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** proof-derived signals, candidate
   selection, leased stochastic authorship, independent validation, local
   absorption, authority continuity, process succession, and readiness proof.
2. **Missing bridge:** one configured originating-deficiency-to-post-successor-
   outcome loop, with recovery and repeated-cycle operator policy.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** the resident can participate in producing and
   becoming a measured successor generation without controlling its own verdict.
4. **Still unproven:** autonomy, monotonic improvement, open-ended recursion,
   safe unbounded iteration, or general problem-solving gains.
5. **Evidence:** exact multi-generation causal chains, independent before/after
   acceptance measures, negative-result retention, and failure recovery drills.

### Phase change D — model replacement → causal experiments on persistence

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** independent model identity/provenance,
   frozen current/history context, five-condition experiments, repeated campaigns,
   and local serving custody.
2. **Missing bridge:** production temporal resident A→B→A evidence using
   commissioned nonsynthetic models, plus externally evaluated semantic outcomes.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** model-, history-, software-, and environment-
   associated effects can be experimentally separated.
4. **Still unproven:** a model-independent person, identity continuity, learning,
   or consciousness.
5. **Evidence:** factorial, preregistered, replicated resident trials with exact
   restoration and independent scoring.

### Phase change E — logs → longitudinal evidence-based self-model

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** durable ledgers, proof preservation,
   World-State, developmental history, and deterministic claim-level ingestion and
   reconciliation across generations without reflection overwriting source evidence.
2. **Missing bridge:** configured runtime production after same-tick World-State,
   bounded prior-tick cognitive consumption, and independent calibration trials.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** the system can use its revisable account of
   what it was, what changed, why, and what followed.
4. **Still unproven:** narrative coherence, psychological continuity, or correct
   causal explanation beyond the bound evidence.
5. **Evidence:** generation-spanning reconstruction, contradiction correction,
   source withdrawal, and independent replay yielding the same bounded claims.

### Phase change F — telemetry → purpose-to-physical-consequence accounting

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** causal principal roots, host telemetry,
   component limits, exact effect admissions, and effect receipts.
2. **Missing bridge:** allocator/enforcer/consumption ledgers, trusted physical
   meters, propagation, and consequence feedback.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** purpose can be traced through compute/energy or
   device use to effect and measured consequence, enabling cost-aware adaptation.
4. **Still unproven:** exact attribution for shared hardware, optimal allocation,
   or legitimate conversion of resource efficiency into policy value.
5. **Evidence:** conservation, contention, retry/crash, meter-calibration, effect,
   consequence, and subsequent-use traces under a single verified principal.

### Phase change G — governed components → persistent governed causal organization

1. **Present prerequisites — ESTABLISHED:** separated authority/effect/adoption,
   software and model generations, memory domains, proof, and World-State.
2. **Missing bridge:** comprehensive composition across the selected live domains,
   explicit constitutional policy for repeated development, and operational proof.
3. **After closure — IMPLIED:** cognition, memory, software, model, effects, and
   environment may change while authority and lineage remain attributable.
4. **Still unproven:** completeness against every failure mode, multi-operator
   legitimacy, autonomy, sentience, or moral status.
5. **Evidence:** long-duration, adversarial, generation-spanning operation with
   complete custody, recovery, and operator-auditable causal reconstruction.

## 5. Capability envelopes

### 5.1 Present capability — ESTABLISHED

Subject to each path's configuration, authority, platform, and environment:

* The launched `sentientosd` can run maintenance evidence collection, build
  World-State, and expose read-only host review; optional maintenance and
  developmental owners require exact configuration.
* The local chat service can use commissioned and activated local models with
  per-generation inference admission and durable conversation identity. Models,
  weights, compute, and operator activation must exist.
* A configured developmental owner can turn allowed World-State facts into
  immutable history and, on later ticks, consume bounded prior history. Optional
  present/withheld/restored comparison records digest-level effects only.
* Explicit experiments can compare two already-governed cognitive endpoints
  while holding current context and history fixed, and campaigns can repeat the
  preregistered comparison. They do not hot-swap production cognition.
* The bounded maintenance topology can admit leased candidate work, allow
  stochastic authorship, independently validate, adopt a local Git successor,
  preserve same-or-narrower authority, and perform POSIX resident replacement
  with post-exec readiness when the full configuration and operator authority
  are supplied.
* Governed effect paths can perform exact admitted filesystem, diagnostic,
  subprocess, process, local-model, and configured external-network effects.
  Actuator existence is never a default grant or evidence an effect occurred.
* Audit, proof, receipt, lineage, and World-State tools can verify and project
  bounded evidence. Federation remains operator-invoked/laboratory rather than
  an established production synchronized system.

### 5.2 Near-term latent capability — IMPLIED after identifiable closure

* **Proof-grounded resident diagnosis:** authenticated World-State already reaches
  bounded cognition; claim-level self-model write/reconciliation and calibration
  would close the diagnostic loop.
* **Attributable longitudinal development:** immutable writeback, later retrieval,
  and intervention already exist; admitted selective distillation and sustained
  trials would close a more meaningful retention loop.
* **Measured recursive software succession:** the constitutive maintenance chain
  exists; binding originating signal to post-successor consequence and recovery
  would close the improvement loop.
* **Resident cognitive succession experiments:** serving custody and controlled
  comparison exist; real A→B→A resident transition plus independent semantic
  measurement would close the intervention.
* **Generation-spanning proof preservation:** transition journals and durable
  proofs exist; a unified read model spanning software/model/developmental
  generations would make reconstruction routinely consumable.

### 5.3 Architecturally implied capability — IMPLIED, important work remains

* A persistent causal organization can remain experimentally identifiable while
  its cognitive model and resident implementation change independently.
* Recursive improvement can be made auditable because proposal, measurement,
  adoption, and subsequent observation belong to different custody domains.
* Longitudinal self-knowledge can be both developmentally influential and
  correctable because memory and current truth are separate inputs.
* Purpose, authority, resource use, effect, and consequence can eventually be
  joined without allowing any one link to impersonate another.
* Evidence and candidate improvements can cross a federation boundary while
  adoption remains local, provided live transport and local review close.

### 5.4 Speculative possibility — HYPOTHESIS

* Stable, useful behavioral dispositions may persist across replacement of the
  model that expresses them because history and system organization persist.
* The system may discover which developmental experiences transfer across model
  families and which are artifact-specific.
* Repeated proof-grounded modification may improve reliability over long horizons
  without eroding authority boundaries.
* Consequence- and resource-aware development may yield better strategies under
  real physical constraints.
* A robust longitudinal identity concept might become operationally useful.

None of these hypotheses establishes consciousness, sentience, autonomous moral
agency, open-ended self-improvement, or a right to act. Architecture can make
such questions measurable; it cannot answer them by naming components.

## 6. Additional relationships revealed by composition

Several architectural consequences are easy to miss when mechanisms are read in
isolation:

* **Rollback is epistemic as well as operational.** Restoring model A or a prior
  software generation is not merely recovery; exact restoration is a control
  condition needed to distinguish intervention effects from drift. Current model
  experiments include restoration checks, while software rollback is not yet
  universal.
* **Quiescence is a causal boundary.** Stopping the predecessor before successor
  readiness prevents two generations from ambiguously owning cadence and evidence.
  This is as important for attribution as for process safety.
* **Negative results are developmental assets.** A validated change that fails to
  improve the originating measure should remain attributable evidence. Otherwise
  recursive work selects only flattering narratives and cannot calibrate itself.
* **Conflict is information, not merely dirt.** World-State retains contradictions;
  developmental cognition can consume applicable conflicts. A future self-model
  should preserve disagreement rather than resolve it through model confidence.
* **Privacy is part of identity continuity.** Separating canonical user memory
  from developmental history prevents “continuity” from becoming an unlimited
  claim on retained personal data. Any bridge between them is constitutional.
* **Same-or-narrower authority is inheritance without privilege reproduction.**
  Software succession carries enough authority to continue admitted work but
  cannot use succession itself to expand that authority.
* **Evidence is the inter-generation language.** Models and implementations can
  differ, but canonical digests, receipts, stages, and source identities provide
  a substrate successors can verify without trusting predecessor narration.
* **The system is designed for interventions, not just upgrades.** Separating
  history, model, training claims, software, environment, and authority makes
  controlled causal questions possible: which variable changed, what stayed
  fixed, and which observation followed?

## 7. What is SentientOS?

### 30-second explanation

SentientOS is an operator-governed runtime and evidence architecture for a
persistent local system whose memory, cognitive model, software, and environment
may change. It keeps proposals separate from permissions, memories separate from
current facts, and model judgments separate from deterministic validation. Its
distinctive aim is not to make a model sovereign; it is to make change—including
change partly authored by cognition—observable, attributable, and adoptable only
through explicit custody.

### 5-minute explanation

The system has several partial causal loops. Evidence from tests, audits, host
observation, and lifecycle records is projected into a bounded World-State. A
configured developmental path can consume that present evidence, write immutable
interpretations, and allow only later ticks to retrieve them. Controlled
withholding and restoration can test whether retained history changes observable
cognition.

Separately, failures and gaps can become proposal-only improvement signals. An
operator-derived lease may permit stochastic machinery to author a candidate in
isolation. Deterministic controllers—not that machinery—reconstruct the change,
run required validation, adopt an exact repository generation, preserve only
same-or-narrower authority, replace the resident process, and demand readiness
from the successor. This is most of the machinery for recursive software
succession, but the full deficiency-to-post-successor-consequence loop is not a
demonstrated default autonomous cycle.

Cognitive models have their own custody. Because active model identity,
developmental history, current World-State, software generation, and reported
training lineage are separated, model replacement can become a controlled
intervention. Existing experiments compare frozen conditions across endpoints;
real resident model succession and semantic longitudinal evidence remain missing.

Governance binds these loops. Cognition may interpret, propose, or author, but it
cannot make its belief into a grant, its intention into an effect, its output into
validation, or its candidate into adoption. This “hard shell / soft interior” is
what allows development without granting the developing component control of the
measurement and authority systems that judge it.

### Expert explanation

The persistent object is a set of independently versioned causal dimensions:
runtime generation, Git/software generation, commissioned cognitive artifact,
reported model-development provenance, immutable developmental record set,
canonical user-memory domain, current evidence projection, authority state,
effect/consequence history, and emerging causal-resource lineage. Durable
digests, ledgers, transition journals, and receipts let later generations verify
bounded predecessor claims without inheriting predecessor belief as truth.

World-State is the current-evidence plane. Memory is the retained-interpretation
plane. Cognition operates over projections of both but owns neither current truth
nor effect authority. Improvement machinery turns evidence into proposals;
isolated stochastic authorship is followed by deterministic validation and
adoption. Software succession preserves state, authority, and execution continuity
as different predicates. Cognitive-model experiments preserve model, history,
environment, and software as different variables. Effect custody preserves
intent, admission, attempt, observed outcome, rollback, and adoption as different
events. Causal resource principals begin a parallel account of whose purpose
caused work, without confusing sponsorship with entitlement or permission.

Composed fully, these planes form a recursive experimental architecture: the
system can observe a bounded condition, retain an attributable interpretation,
propose and validate a change, adopt a successor, and measure what followed while
keeping the author, judge, authority source, actuator, and historian distinguishable.
Today, this statement is partly **ESTABLISHED** at individual and bounded composed
paths and partly **IMPLIED** at the whole-loop level.

### Architectural thesis

SentientOS appears to be attempting to preserve accountable continuity through
change. Its deepest design choice is to place identity in causal organization
rather than in a single model, process, memory file, or repository checkout. The
system can therefore contemplate replacing cognition and software without
discarding history, authority, or the evidence required to compare generations.

Its many boundaries are not bureaucratic duplication. They are an experimental
and constitutional structure for recursion. A stochastic component may remain
open-ended in how it interprets and proposes, while deterministic mechanisms
control what becomes durable, executable, authoritative, or adopted. The same
separation makes causal experiments possible: history can be withheld, models
can be exchanged, software can advance, environments can vary, and the other
dimensions can in principle be held fixed.

The project therefore represents a move from “an application containing an AI
model” toward “a governed persistent causal organization with replaceable
cognition.” That latter object is not fully closed today. The credible trajectory
is toward an operator-accountable developmental system whose changes and
consequences can be reconstructed across generations. Claims beyond that—general
autonomy, guaranteed self-improvement, stable personhood, consciousness, or
sentience—remain unsupported.
